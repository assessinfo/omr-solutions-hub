Who We Are

Trusted OMR & Outsourcing Partner Since 2004

OMR India Outsources Pvt. Ltd. (OIPL), established in 2004 by serial entrepreneur Rohit Gupta, is a distinguished member of the IOS Group. We specialise in delivering comprehensive IT and outsourcing solutions across examination management, digital evaluation, and data analytics.

We are proud partners with leading global organisations — including Gravic Inc., USA for Remark Office OMR Software, and Sekonic Corporation, Japan for state-of-the-art OMR Scanners — bringing world-class technology to Indian institutions and enterprises.
