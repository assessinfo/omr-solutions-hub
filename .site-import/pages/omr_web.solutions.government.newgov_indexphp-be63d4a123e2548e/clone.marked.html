Government, Wise

Exam sheets, recruitment drives and survey forms — printed, scanned, captured and evaluated end-to-end, with a clean merit list at the finish.

Get a Quote Call +91-9319123429

Government departments handle a constant stream of paperwork — population surveys, KYC verification, and recruitment exams for Class I, II and III posts, each with its own application and admit-card process, online or offline.

OMR Solutions takes this off your hands: we print, scan, capture and evaluate OMR sheets end-to-end, and deliver an accurate merit list — cutting the manual labour, long hours and risk of error that come with processing thousands of forms by hand.
